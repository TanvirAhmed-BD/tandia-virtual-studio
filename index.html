About the Studio

Tandia Virtual Studio was founded by Tanvir Ahmed in Chattogram, Bangladesh. Before going independent, he spent more than ten years in administration, accounting assistance and data operations in Oman and Cambodia — work where accuracy is not optional.

Today the studio brings that same discipline to clients around the world: books formatted to the page, captions written to be posted, resumes built to be read. Everything is delivered remotely, in clear English, with honest timelines.
